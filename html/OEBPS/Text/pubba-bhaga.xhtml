Pubba-bhāga

〈 Namo tassa 〉

Bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa [3x]

〈 Handa mayaṁ sacca-kiriyā-gāthāyo bhaṇāmase 〉

N'atthi me saraṇaṁ aññaṁ
Buddho me saraṇaṁ varaṁ

Etena sacca-vajjena
Sotthi me hotu sabbadā.

N'atthi me saraṇaṁ aññaṁ
Dhammo me saraṇaṁ varaṁ

Etena sacca-vajjena
Sotthi me hotu sabbadā.

N'atthi me saraṇaṁ aññaṁ
Saṅgho me saraṇaṁ varaṁ

Etena sacca-vajjena
Sotthi me hotu sabbadā.

[Trad]

Preliminary Part

Homage to the Blessed Worthy and Perfectly Enlightened One [3x]

〈 Now let us recite the act-of-truth verses: 〉

There's no other refuge for me, the Buddha is my foremost refuge
By the saying of this truth, may I always be well.

There's no other refuge for me, the Dhamma is my foremost refuge
By the saying of this truth, may I always be well.

There's no other refuge for me, the Saṅgha is my foremost refuge
By the saying of this truth, may I always be well.

[Trad]
