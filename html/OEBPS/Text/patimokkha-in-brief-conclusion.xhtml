The Pātimokkha Recitation in Brief Conclusion

Level 1

The Nidāna is recited in full; the rest is announced as heard.

Venerables, the introduction has been recited. Heard by the venerables have been the four cases involving disqualification, the thirteen cases [involving] the community in the beginning and in the rest, the two indefinite cases, the thirty cases involving expiation with forfeiture, the ninety-two cases involving expiation, the four cases that are to be acknowledged, the seventy-five cases related to the training, the seven cases that are settlements of legal issues. This much [training-rule] of the Fortunate One has been handed down in the Sutta, has been included in the Sutta, comes up for recitation half-monthly. By all who are united, who are on friendly terms, who are not disputing, is to be trained herein.

The Bhikkhu Disciplinary Code has been finished
