Sīl'uddesa-pāṭha

〈 Handa mayaṁ sīl'uddesa-pāṭhaṁ bhaṇāmase 〉

Bhāsitam'idaṁ tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena:

“Sampanna-sīlā bhikkhave viharatha sampanna-pātimokkhā.

Pātimokkha-saṁvara-saṁvutā viharatha ācāra-gocara-sampannā.

Aṇumattesu vajjesu bhaya-dassāvī samādāya sikkhatha sikkhāpadesū'ti.

Tasmā't'ih'amhehi sikkhitabbaṁ.

“Sampanna-sīlā viharissāma sampanna-pātimokkhā.

Pātimokkha-saṁvara-saṁvutā viharissāma ācāra-gocara-sampannā.

Aṇumattesu vajjesu bhaya-dassāvī samādāya sikkhissāma sikkhāpadesū'ti.

Evañ'hi no sikkhitabbaṁ.

[MN 6]

The Summary on Virtue

〈 Let us recite the summary on virtue: 〉

This was said by the Blessed One, the One who Knows, the One who Sees, the Worthy One, the Perfectly Enlightened One:

“Live consummate in virtue, bhikkhus, and consummate in the Pātimokkhā.

Live restrained with the restrain of the Pātimokkhā, consummate in your behaviour and sphere of activity.

Train yourselves, having undertaken the training rules, seeing danger in the slightest faults.”

Therefore we should train ourselves:

“We will live consummate in virtue, consummate in the Pātimokkhā.

We will live restrained with the restrain of the Pātimokkhā, consummate in our behaviour and sphere of activity.

We will train ourselves, having undertaken the training rules, seeing danger in the slightest faults.”

That's how we should train ourselves.
